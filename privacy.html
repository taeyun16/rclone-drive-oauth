Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes the personal rclone Google Drive OAuth configuration linked from this site.

Google data accessed and why

When the account owner grants access, rclone can list and read Google Drive files and metadata and can create, update, or delete files available to that account. This access is used to show and operate on those files from the owner’s computer.

How data is handled

rclone runs on the user’s device and communicates with Google Drive. Files may be read, written, or cached locally, and file names or errors may appear in local logs. OAuth configuration and tokens are stored on the device to maintain the connection. This personal configuration does not use a separate operator-hosted service to store Google Drive data or tokens.

The user controls their own local files and any other software that accesses the mounted drive. Google processes data according to its own policies. GitHub Pages hosts this static website and may process normal web request logs according to GitHub’s policies. This website does not contain analytics scripts or a form that collects Google Drive data.

Retention and control

The Google account owner can revoke the OAuth grant through Google Account permissions. Local configuration, logs, and cached files remain on the device until the user removes them. Before deleting a cache, the user should make sure that no uploads are pending.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen for this application.
