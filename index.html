Personal utility

rclone

Personal Google Drive access

This personal configuration uses rclone to connect the owner’s Google Drive to their own computer.

With the Google account owner’s consent, rclone can list, read, create, change, and delete Google Drive files and their metadata. The connection is intended for personal use; it is not a public file-sharing service.

The application runs on the user’s computer. There is no separate application server operated for storing Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Privacy policy

For questions about this OAuth application, use the support email shown on its Google consent screen.
